# Top Goats: Premium Copywriting Service Model

## 1. Overview: The "Independent Label" Standard
The Premium Copywriting Service is designed to give underground artists the professional "polish" typically reserved for major label rosters. By providing access to experienced music copywriters, Top Goats helps artists translate their sonic identity into a compelling narrative that resonates with fans, journalists, and playlist curators.

## 2. Core Service Offerings

### A. The "Herd" Artist Bio
*   **Purpose:** Narrative storytelling for Spotify, social media profiles, and website "About" pages.
*   **Format:** Short (150 words) and Standard (300-500 words) versions.
*   **Focus:** Origin story, sonic influences, key achievements, and current "vibe."

### B. Release Press Releases
*   **Purpose:** Formal announcements for single, EP, or album launches.
*   **Format:** Standard PR structure (Headline, Dateline, Intro, Body, About Artist, Contact).
*   **Focus:** Why this release matters, technical highlights, and quotes.

### C. The "Sovereign" EPK Copy
*   **Purpose:** A comprehensive text package for Electronic Press Kits.
*   **Includes:** Updated bio, "For Fans Of" (FFO) section, blurb for the latest release, and boilerplate contact info.

### Top Goats Strategy:
*   **Bulk Freelancing:** Top Goats partners with a stable of vetted freelance music copywriters, guaranteeing them volume in exchange for lower wholesale rates ($25-$30 per bio).
*   **AI-Assisted Hybrid:** For lower-tier/fast-turnaround requests, an internal AI model generates a draft based on artist data, which is then refined by a human editor for a "high-touch" feel at a "low-touch" cost.

## 4. Request & Delivery Workflow

The service is managed entirely through the Top Goats dashboard to ensure sovereignty and data security.

1.  **Request (Artist):**
    *   Artist navigates to "Services" > "Copywriting."
    *   Fills out the **Vibe Survey**:
        *   3 Genre keywords.
        *   3 "Sounds Like" (FFO) artists.
        *   Links to 1-2 tracks (unreleased or current).
        *   Brief origin story/key message.
2.  **Assignment (Platform):**
    *   Request is routed to a copywriter with expertise in the artist's genre.
3.  **Drafting (Copywriter):**
    *   Copywriter delivers the first draft within **72 hours**.
4.  **Review & Revision (Artist):**
    *   Artist receives a notification to review the draft in-app.
    *   Includes **one round of revisions** for tone and factual accuracy.
5.  **Final Delivery:**
    *   Final copy is approved and archived in the artist's "Media Kit" folder on the platform.
    *   Available as plain text, Markdown, and formatted PDF.

## 5. Implementation Roadmap
*   **Phase 1 (Beta):** Manual assignment to 5 core freelance copywriters.
*   **Phase 2 (MVP):** Integration of the Vibe Survey form and automated routing.
*   **Phase 3 (Scale):** AI-drafting tool for "Kid" and "Yearling" tiers to provide instant, low-cost bio skeletons.
