## 3. How Tiers Complement Herd Ranks (Not Replace)

The Herd (Kid → Yearling → Ram → Great Goat → Top Goat) is **earned through XP** — social engagement, platform activity, commercial success, community participation. It's merit-based.

Premium tiers are **paid** — an artist can buy into a tier at any time regardless of Herd rank.

**Interaction model:**
- An artist at **Kid** rank can pay for **Yearling Sound** ($9.99/mo) → gets access to services but Herd-tier perks (like lower fees from rank) are capped at Kid level
- An artist who has **earned Ram** rank AND pays for **Ram Sound** → gets combined benefits: the rank fee + the premium fee reduction stack
- An artist who is a **Top Goat** (earned) but pays for **Yearling Sound** → gets Top Goat's earned 2.0% fee + Yearling's services

**Simple rule:** Herd = earned fee reduction. Premium = services + bonus fee reduction. They complement.

| Scenario | Herd Rank Fee | Premium Tier Fee | Effective Fee |
|----------|--------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Kid + No Premium | 10.0% | — | **10.0%** |
| Kid + Yearling Sound | 10.0% | 8.0% (replaces) | **8.0%** |
| Yearling (earned) + No Premium | 8.0% | — | **8.0%** |
| Yearling + Ram Sound | — | 6.0% (overrides) | **6.0%** |
| Ram (earned) + Ram Sound | 6.0% | 6.0% (matches, doesn't stack lower) | **6.0%** |
| Great Goat + Great Goat Sound | 4.0% | 4.0% | **4.0%** |

## 5. Recommendation — What to Launch With

### Phase 1 (Now — with $9.99/mo Premium):
- **Yearling Sound ($9.99/mo)** + 3 a-la-carte products: Copyright Filing ($50), Bio Copywriting ($75), Promo Boost Light ($25)
- Keep it simple — validate that artists pay for premium before building out the ladder

### Phase 2 (Month 2-3):
- Add **Ram Sound ($19.99/mo)** — distribution included via Gyrostream once integrated
- Add 5 more a-la-carte products

### Phase 3 (Month 4-6):
- Add **Great Goat Sound ($29.99/mo)** + sync licensing
- Add remaining a-la-carte catalog
- **Top Goat Sound ($49.99/mo)** launches when first artists reach Top Goat earned rank — this is aspirational and shouldn't be available until someone actually reaches it

### Why $19.99 is the sweet spot:
- SoundCloud Next Pro is ~$12/mo — we're above that because we include distribution ($10-$15 value as standalone)
- DistroKid Musician Plus is $3.33/mo but doesn't include any services, just distribution
- $19.99 bundles distribution + copywriting + copyright protection + Herd gamification — compelling value
- At 50 artists on Ram Sound: $999.50/mo recurring revenue, minus ~$149 Gyrostream = **$850.50/mo margin**
