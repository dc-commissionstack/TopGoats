## 2. Headquarters Strategy: Berlin (The Sonic Hub)
After researching Berlin, London, and LA, **Berlin** is selected as the primary headquarters location due to its cultural alignment with underground electronic music and competitive operational costs.

### Location: Kreuzberg or Neukölln
*   **Cultural Fit**: Home to the world's most influential underground clubs (Berghain, Tresor) and a massive density of experimental music producers.
*   **Office Space**: 150 sqm Creative Loft / Office.
*   **Est. Monthly Rent**: €4,500 - €6,000 (inclusive of utilities and internet).
*   **Initial Setup CapEx**: €25,000 (Furniture, soundproof meeting rooms, hardware).
