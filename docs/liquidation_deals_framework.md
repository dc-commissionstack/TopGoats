# Top Goats: Flash Liquidation Deals Framework & Artist Agreements

## 1. Executive Summary
The "Flash Liquidation Deal" is a platform-wide event where digital music prices are temporarily dropped (typically to $1.00 per song) to drive massive consumer engagement, platform volume, and new user acquisition. This framework outlines the legal, technical, and marketing strategies to ensure these events benefit both the platform and its sovereign artist community.

## 2. Legal & Technical Framework
The framework is built on the principle of **Artist Sovereignty**, ensuring that while the platform drives volume, the artist retains control over their participation.

### A. Core Terms of Service (ToS)
The platform's master ToS will include a "Promotional Events" clause:
*   **Grant of Rights**: Artists grant Top Goats the non-exclusive right to include their digital catalog in platform-wide promotional events, subject to the notification and opt-out procedures defined herein.
*   **Price Floor**: A mandatory "Liquidation Floor" is set at $1.00 USD per track. No automated deal will ever price music below this threshold without explicit, separate artist consent.

### B. Agreement & Consent (Scalability)
To manage agreements at scale across thousands of artists, Top Goats uses a **Tiered Consent Model**:
1.  **Kid & Yearling Tiers**: Automatically enrolled in platform-wide deals to help them gain initial traction. They receive an "Opt-Out" notification 7 days prior to any event.
2.  **Ram, Great Goat, & Top Goat Tiers**: Due to their higher market value, these tiers operate on an **Explicit Opt-In** basis. They must check a "Participate in Next Liquidation" box in their dashboard.
3.  **Global Toggle**: Every artist profile features a permanent "Enable Liquidation Participation" toggle that can be switched off at any time to prevent inclusion in future events.

### C. Notification Workflow
*   **T-Minus 14 Days**: General announcement of the upcoming liquidation date.
*   **T-Minus 7 Days**: Direct notification (Email/Push) to all eligible artists with a summary of the deal terms.
*   **T-Minus 48 Hours**: Final lock-in period. Opt-outs must be processed before this window.

## 3. Financial Model
During liquidation events, the transaction model shifts to prioritize volume:
*   **Revenue Split**: The platform may choose to waive its 2-5% transaction fee during the 24-hour peak of a liquidation event to maximize the artist’s take-home pay on $1 sales.
*   **Transaction Batching**: To minimize Stripe processing fees on $1 transactions, Top Goats will utilize "Cart Batching," encouraging consumers to buy multiple tracks in a single transaction.

## 4. Marketing & Consumer Engagement
To drive massive volume without devaluing the music long-term, the deals are marketed as **high-scarcity community events**.

### A. The "Herd Stampede" Brand
*   The event is branded as the "Herd Stampede"—a rare, high-energy event for "true fans" to support underground artists.
*   **Visual Urgency**: A live "Stampede Countdown" on the homepage and high-contrast "Liquidation" badges on participating artist pages.

### B. Artist Promotion Toolkit
*   Participating artists receive a "Stampede Kit": pre-sized social media graphics (Instagram Stories, Twitter banners) and a unique "Liquidation Link" that takes fans directly to their $1 catalog.
*   **XP Bonuses**: Artists earn double "Herd XP" for every sale made during the liquidation window, incentivizing them to drive their own traffic.

### C. Consumer Incentives
*   **Early Access**: Premium subscribers get 2-hour early access to the liquidation deals before they go public.
*   **Gamified Rewards**: Consumers who purchase more than 5 tracks during a Stampede receive a "Supporter" badge on their profile.

## 5. Devaluation Protection
To ensure that $1 sales do not become the "expected" price for music:
*   **Strict Frequency**: Liquidation events are limited to a maximum of 4 times per year (quarterly).
*   **Post-Event Reset**: Prices automatically revert to the artist's original set price immediately upon the conclusion of the event.
*   **Narrative**: Marketing copy emphasizes that "Liquidation" is a gift from the artist to the community, not a standard price drop.

## 6. References & Industry Benchmarks
The "Herd Stampede" model is informed by the following successful digital marketplace strategies:
*   **Bandcamp Fridays**: Demonstrated that scheduled, time-limited promotional events can drive significant revenue directly to artists ($120M+ generated via these events as of 2024).
*   **Steam Seasonal Sales**: Proved that deep discounting in digital marketplaces can lead to a net increase in revenue and user acquisition through massive volume, even when individual unit prices are low.
*   **Stripe "Large Volume" Processing**: Benchmarks for micro-transaction optimization, specifically the "cart batching" approach to mitigate the $0.30 fixed fee on small transactions.
