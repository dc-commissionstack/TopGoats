# Copyright Tech Deep Dive & Niche Outreach Strategy

## 1. Copyright Tech: ACRCloud vs. Audible Magic

| Feature | **ACRCloud** | **Audible Magic** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Use** | Music recognition, Humming, Broadcast Monitoring. | Rights management, Content identification for UGC. |
| **Pricing Model** | Transparent tiered pricing (Free -> Paid). | Enterprise-focused (Contact Sales). |
| **Estimated Cost** | **Free Tier:** 100 queries/day.<br>**Paid:** ~$10/mo for 1,000 queries.<br>**Scale:** ~$0.01 per additional query. | **Base:** Often $1,000+/mo for commercial platforms.<br>**Startup Program:** Negotiable but higher entry bar. |
| **Tech Requirements** | REST API, SDKs for Android/iOS/Web. Simple JSON responses. | REST API, requires legal compliance and often "Notice and Takedown" workflows. |
| **Database Size** | 70M+ tracks. | 100M+ tracks (Major label catalog depth is superior). |

**Recommendation:** For the MVP, **ACRCloud** is the clear choice due to its low entry cost, transparent pricing, and ease of integration for "Hello World" developers.

## 2. Top 5 Underground Subcultures for Beta Users

To find our first 100 beta users, we will target communities that value sovereignty and already "hack" existing systems:

1.  **Hyperpop/Glitchcore (Discord/Reddit):** High concentration of independent producer-vocalists who are extremely tech-savvy and cynical about major labels.
2.  **Phonk & Drift Phonk (Discord/Telegram):** A rapidly growing DIY subculture. Many artists use unlicensed samples and need a path to formalize their sovereignty.
3.  **Lo-fi / Chillhop Producers (Reddit r/makinghiphop):** Independent beatmakers who rely on direct-to-fan sales and are often targeted by fraudulent copyright claims.
4.  **Experimental / Noise / Left-field Electronic (Bandcamp-heavy):** These artists prioritize creative sovereignty and are looking for Bandcamp alternatives that offer more protection.
5.  **KTT2 (KanyeToThe successor):** A general underground hip-hop/pop forum with a high density of early adopters and influential "tastemakers" who follow independent artists.
