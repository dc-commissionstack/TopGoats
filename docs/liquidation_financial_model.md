# Flash Liquidation & SSF Financial Modeling

## 1. The "Herd Stampede" Financial Model ($1.00/Song)
The primary goal of Flash Liquidation deals is not immediate profit, but **Massive User Acquisition (UA)** and **Velocity**. By dropping the price to $1.00, we lower the barrier to entry for fans to "start their collection" on Top Goats.

### Transaction Fee Optimization
Standard Stripe fees (2.9% + $0.30) are unsustainable for $1.00 items (33% fee). We will implement two technical mitigations:
1.  **Microtransaction Pricing**: Switch to Stripe's 5% + $0.05 model for transactions under $5.00.
    *   *Result*: $0.10 total fee (10%).
2.  **Cart Batching**: Encouraging fans to "Fill the Crate." If a fan buys 5+ songs, the transaction moves to standard pricing (2.9% + $0.30), which is more efficient for larger totals.
    *   *Example*: 10 songs for $10.00 -> $0.59 fee (5.9% total).

### Revenue Split during Liquidation
To keep artists happy and engaged, Top Goats will adopt a "Volume-First" split:
*   **Artist Share**: 90% of Net ($0.81 per song on micro-pricing).
*   **Platform Share**: 10% of Net ($0.09 per song).

## 2. Impact on Sovereign Sound Foundation (SSF)
The business plan specifies a 1.0% 'Sovereignty Fee' on platform profits. During liquidation, "profit" per transaction is slim. To ensure the SSF remains visible and impactful, we propose a **Liquidation Bonus Model**.

### Proposed SSF Model for "Herd Stampede" Events:
*   **The "Pillar Match"**: During the 24-hour event, Top Goats will donate **10% of its platform share** to the SSF (instead of 1%).
    *   *Contribution*: ~$0.01 per song sold.
*   **Branding Benefit**: This allows us to market the event with: *"Every $1 song helps protect an independent artist's rights. 10% of platform fees go directly to the SSF IP Grant Fund."*

## 3. Volume and Revenue Projections
To reach "Revenue Positive" status while supporting a staff of 15 and an HQ, we must scale MAU rapidly.

*   **Required Monthly Revenue**: $150,000+.

**Conclusion**: Liquidation deals are the "top-of-funnel" engine to reach the 240,000+ MAU needed to maintain 12,000 Premium subscribers.

## 4. Frequency and Duration
To maximize engagement without devaluing music:
*   **Frequency**: Quarterly (4 times per year).
    *   *Alignment*: Seasonal shifts (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter/Holiday).
*   **Duration**: 24 to 48 hours maximum.
    *   *Psychology*: "Flash" nature creates urgency. Extended durations lead to "wait for the sale" consumer behavior which devalues the artist's work.

## 5. Artist Agreement Model (Opt-In/Opt-Out)
Sovereignty is the core brand. Participation must be transparent.

### Technical Implementation:
*   **Auto-Enrollment (Kid/Yearling)**: Lower-tier artists are auto-enrolled to gain them initial visibility. They receive a dashboard notification 10 days prior with a "One-Click Opt-Out."
*   **Active Opt-In (Ram/Great Goat/Top Goat)**: High-tier artists must actively opt-in. We provide them with a "Stampede Analytics" preview showing projected earnings based on their current "Herd" size.
