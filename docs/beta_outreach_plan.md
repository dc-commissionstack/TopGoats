### Hyperpop Scene Connections
- **tashka** is a noted frequent collaborator — known for working with multiple artists in the Hyperpop/Glitchcore space. Could be a bridge to others.
- **Infinideaux** appears on similar Bandcamp emerging release lists as tashka.
- **fur:trash** is part of the glitchcore collective scene that overlaps with tashka's network.
- **Strategy:** Reach out to tashka first as a potential "connector" who can vouch for the platform within the Hyperpop scene.

- Minimal direct cross-pollination between Phonk and Hyperpop artists, but the shared "sovereignty" message applies to both.
- **Recommendation:** Send the same core message with scene-specific references (e.g., mention "Phonk scene" vs "Hyperpop scene") rather than trying to force cross-tribe connections.

| **"What's in it for me?"** — need to see immediate value | Lead with Free Premium for Life and 4.0% fee lock. Concrete numbers. |
| **Platform not ready for transactions** — buy buttons show "Coming Soon" | Be transparent: invite them to shape the commercial launch. Early access = power. |
