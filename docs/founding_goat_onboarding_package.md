## 2. SSF Fiscal Sponsorship Application Draft
*Target Organization: Fractured Atlas / Center for Cultural Innovation*

### Project Title: Sovereign Sound Foundation (SSF)
**Mission Statement:** To empower, protect, and sustain the sovereignty of the independent music creator by providing the legal, financial, and mental health resources necessary to thrive outside the traditional label system.

**Project Description:**  
The Sovereign Sound Foundation (SSF) serves as the philanthropic arm of the Top Goats music platform. It is designed to provide a "sovereignty safety net" for underground and off-label artists who lack the institutional support of major labels. The project focuses on three core pillars:
1.  **Intellectual Property Defense:** Providing micro-grants for formal US Copyright Office filings and maintaining automated fingerprinting tools to prevent IP theft.
2.  **Artist Welfare:** Partnering with mental health organizations (e.g., Backline, MusiCares) to provide subsidized counseling and emergency crisis aid.
3.  **Independent Arts Advocacy:** Funding legal contract reviews and educational resources to help artists navigate the digital economy without sacrificing their autonomy.

**Community Impact:**  
By integrating a 1.0% "Sovereignty Fee" into every commercial transaction on the Top Goats platform, the SSF creates a sustainable, community-funded engine for artist support. This ensures that as the underground scene grows commercially, its most vulnerable creators are protected and provided for.

## 3. Founding Goat 'Sovereignty Agreement'

**Artist Rights & Autonomy**  
The Artist retains 100% ownership of all master recordings and intellectual property uploaded to the Platform. Top Goats serves strictly as a distribution and commercial toolkit. The Artist has the right to set their own pricing and remove their catalog at any time.

**SSF Contribution Transparency**  
By participating in the Top Goats ecosystem, the Artist agrees that a 1.0% "Sovereignty Fee" from the Platform's profit share (not the Artist's share) will be contributed to the Sovereign Sound Foundation (SSF) to fund community IP grants and mental health aid.

**Herd Stampede (Flash Liquidation) Terms**  
1.  **Participation:** As a Founding Goat, you are eligible for platform-wide $1.00/song "Flash Liquidation" events.
2.  **Opt-Out Sovereignty:** Participation is voluntary. Artists will receive notification 7 days prior to any "Stampede" event and have the right to opt-out of that specific event via their dashboard toggle at any time up to 48 hours before the event start.
3.  **Price Floor:** No automated deal will ever price the Artist's music below the $1.00 floor without explicit additional consent.
